✅ What flows forward

Verified tips trade the shadow books; the analyst's take can arm a plan or mint a proposal; every decision is journaled and reviewable.

⛔ What never crosses

Context chatter never becomes a tip on its own. Experiment samples never touch orders, books, proposals, dedupe or scorecards — forced onto the replay path by construction.
Two analysts, two brains: the Tips Analyst's knowledge lives in tip_notes; EM's lives in its own rulebook/prompts. A static guard test fails the suite on any cross-read (PLATFORM-RULES invariant 12).

2 · Knowledge that ages honestly

The research is blunt: stale memory misleads models, and similarity search can't tell a contradiction from a rephrase. So knowledge here has a lifecycle — layered retention (FinMem), temporal invalidation instead of deletion (Zep), LLM-judged audits.

🔁 Citation refresh

A note injected into a completed live appraisal gets cited_count++ and its TTL extended. Knowledge that keeps participating in decisions stays alive; knowledge nobody uses ages out. Historical experiments deliberately never refresh.

🕰 Query-time expiry

Nothing is deleted or swept. An expired note simply stops being served — deterministic, restart-proof, and fully visible under the Knowledge tab's history toggle (superseded + expired).

⚖ Widened audit

Weekly, the same judge→apply pass runs over the rulebook and every ticker/source/general group with ≥3 notes: merge near-duplicates, expire the unsupported, flag real contradictions needs-your-call.

3 · The historical experiment — testing the desk against the past

The published field has no rigorous answer for replay-testing an LLM tips desk — so this loop is the evidence. It grades the process (extraction, verification, knowledge, tools, reasoning), never the P&L: hindsight is cheap, process is fixable.

📋 Rubric batch reviewone run grades all 20: extraction · verification · knowledge injected · tool use · verdict-at-tip-time · gaps → findings note + fixes

🔒 Out-of-band, proven

Zero orders, zero proposals, zero book entries, zero dedupe/scorecard contamination — asserted by tests, not assumed (PLATFORM-RULES invariant 13). The experiment can never move money.

👓 Look-ahead honesty

Two traps the design dodges: live tools seeing today's market (the prompt names it, the review checks for confusion) and the model's own training knowing 2024-25 outcomes (batches sample ≥ Feb 2026 where possible; P&L is never the grade).

Your 5-minute knowledge pass

  • 📅 Read today's digest(s). Wrong lean or missed theme → tune with digest-now before enabling nightly.
  • ⚠ Clear needs-your-call flags. They only appear when two notes genuinely pull opposite ways.
  • ⏳ Glance at expiring notes. Amber chip (≤7d): still true? 📌 pin it. Stale? let it die — that's the system working.
  • 🧪 Experiment cadence. After fixes land, queue the next batch with a new seed; findings go to the build plan.
  • 📌 Teach one thing. Anything you learned watching the rooms today belongs in the composer — scoped, so only the runs that need it see it.

Healthy signs

Rules stay few and sharp · digests promote 0-2 nuggets a day, not 5 · expiring notes mostly deserve to expire · batch reviews find new classes, not repeats.

Smells

Rulebook growing weekly · every note pinned · promotions restating the daily summary · the same extraction bug in two batches — stop and fix before more volume.

5 · How we got here — the thought process

2026-08-27 · The platform bet
Many techniques, one engine. The Tip technique becomes the second resident: extraction → verification → dual shadow books per source, so every tipper earns (or loses) trust on their own record.
2026-08-28 · The analyst is born
Tips deserve judgement, not just parsing: an independent trader persona with market tools, its own exit plans, its own rulebook — explicitly NOT bound by EM's method book.
2026-08-29 · Staying armed, closing gaps
Multi-day stay-armed plans, follow-up handling ("a close never opens"), never-chase caps, nightly retros + the first weekly rule audit. The desk starts maintaining its own judgement.
2026-08-30 · Knowledge grows a lifecycle
Deep research (Zep, FinMem, FinCon + 20 more sources, adversarially verified) settles the design: typed scoped stores, temporal expiry over deletion, citation-refresh, LLM-judged audits, per-agent isolation. Context channels + digests bring trading-floor in without polluting intake. The experiment harness closes the loop: history in, findings out, fixes in, repeat.

Design principles (the short version)

🧭 Process over hindsight

Grade what the desk could have known, never what the chart did later.

🗂 Scope everything

Knowledge reaches only the runs it belongs to; two analysts never share a brain.

⏳ Forget on purpose

Expiry + audits keep the context honest; only cited or pinned knowledge earns permanence.

🔒 Experiments can't spend

Out-of-band by construction, proven by tests — curiosity never risks a dollar.
