## Operating and remaining evidence

After verified deployment, leave research enabled and run fresh Practice preparation
before the session, or use the scheduled run. Read **Validation → Profitability
research** after observations arrive. Earlier preparations are not backfilled as
prospective evidence. Current execution permissions, risk settings and saved
campaigns are unchanged.

portfolio-wide competition among simultaneous entries. The bearish cohort is an
engineering proxy, not the complete March source scanner. Missing quotes or costs
remain unpriced. Promotion needs subsequent sessions, net evidence and a separate
decision under [the protocol](PROFITABILITY-RESEARCH.md).

release. The running build, served UI and restored identities must be checked after
