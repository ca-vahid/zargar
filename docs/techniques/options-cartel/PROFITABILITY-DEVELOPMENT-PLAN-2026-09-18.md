# Options Cartel profitability development plan

Prepared September 18, 2026. Implementation authorized by the user; economic
validation and activation gates remain. This document does not authorize Live trading.

## Outcome and starting point

Determine whether a more faithful, executable interpretation of Sean's method
improves net results after spread, fees, slippage and financing/carry effects.
More signals, more trades, fewer refusals and faster scans are intermediate
measurements, not the success criterion.

The September 18 audit found 13 primary long candidates, five armed names and
no recorded trigger touches among those five. KODK was nearer and touched its
level, but failed the historical-volume coverage gate; it is not a proven missed
executable winner. Across September 8-18, the book recorded one completed APA
campaign, net -$61.13. Its newer fill-quality receipts are missing. Older tapes and
candidate denominators are incomplete; alternative option returns are unknown.

v0.8.20 added opportunity reporting, Practice repair timing improvements and
prospective nearest-level/liquidity research lists. Verify the running build and
effective saved policies before implementation; version numbers are not proof of
readiness. Reuse the existing profitability research framework and decision
bundles rather than creating a second engine or a parallel accounting system.

## Boundaries and responsibilities

- Builder owns implementation, migrations if needed, focused regressions and
  reproducible artifacts. Reviewer checks the full source-to-fill causal path and
  returns one consolidated verdict: code accepted, deployable, and activatable
  are three separate decisions. One agent can perform these roles sequentially.
- Preserve the current control policy, snapshots and campaigns. Every alternative
  has an immutable version, input availability timestamps, and a separate label.
- Research does not create orders, reserve capital or displace active arms.
  Production data, quote, risk, permission, freshness and protective-exit rules
  remain mandatory. Do not solve coverage failures by inventing candles.
- No blanket risk increase, automatic Live activation or imitation of advertised
  returns. Preserve other techniques, their settings, books and runtime work.
- Work in an owned isolated checkout from current main. Run focused tests
  sequentially through scripts/test-codex.ps1 against zargar_test_codex; coordinate
  that database and deployment windows with the other teams.
- Persist study definitions before their evaluation period. Parameter changes
  create a new cohort; never rewrite old results or choose variants after seeing
  their wins. Report failed and abandoned variants too.

## Phase 0 — establish the method contract

**Question:** Which requirements are actually Sean's, which are historical
variants, and which are our hypotheses?

Create a source-to-code matrix covering leadership, base quality, breakout,
undercut/reclaim, 30-minute pivot, confirmation timing, initial stop, shares versus
options, trims and trailing exits. Each row needs source URL, author, publication
date if verified, retrieval date, exact rule interpretation, ambiguity, code path,
effective setting and current test evidence.

Use the archived June strategy for established 5m/15m confirmation and leadership
guidance. Investigate the mirrored entry-model and setup-rating posts; obtain an
original dated post, author video or an independently preserved full copy before
calling their exact numerical rules verified author requirements. Keep unresolved
ideas explicitly experimental. Do not turn “four quarters of revenue growth”
into a hard filter until sequential versus year-over-year growth is resolved.

Deliverables: updated source ledger, method matrix, named variant specifications
and a list of unresolved questions. Preserve source evolution rather than blending
incompatible exit systems or stop rules.

**Exit gate:** every proposed behavior is either supported and versioned or
explicitly identified as an engineering experiment. No implementation depends on
an undisclosed assumption. Unresolved items can remain shadow-only.

## Phase 1 — make opportunity and cost evidence sufficient

**Question:** Are we missing attractive stocks, valid entries, executable options,
or merely hypothetical price moves?

Extend the existing pre-open snapshots and daily review to retain:

- Full eligible population, omitted candidates and research-cap reasons; direction
  and market context; actual arm list and all alternative lists.
- Prior-close trigger distance, stop distance, nearest resistance, structural R,
  relative strength, liquidity, theme proxy and all missing fields.
- Each transition: selected, data-ready, level reached, confirmation passed,
  contract-ready, risk-admitted, order submitted, filled, managed and closed.
  Keep first blocker, independent blockers and later recovery separate.
- Exact input bundles, proof availability, native-bar provenance, observation
  deadlines, restart/correction boundaries and fresh versus recovered signals.
- Option contract identity, quote/delta timestamps, bid/ask, spread in dollars at
  intended quantity, commissions, fills, exit evidence and source-qualified marks.

Audit why there are no profit_quote records in the inspected sample. A day with
no research signal legitimately needs no fill valuation, but missing quote
collection after a valid signal must be an explicit defect or coverage gap.
Do not substitute stock returns for option returns.

Deliverables: one reconciled session report and a multi-session denominator/cost
coverage report. Old sessions retain honest unknowns; do not backdate observations.

**Exit gate:** every studied signal has a traceable disposition; costs are either
measured or explicitly unavailable. Actual account totals reconcile. Economic
comparisons use a common eligible sample and expose excluded/unknown cases.

## Phase 2 — test selection and setup quality independently

**Question:** Does our structural-R-first ranking select the right opportunities?

Keep the current entry, stop and exit rules fixed. Compare existing quality and
leader-first lists with nearest-unbroken and liquidity-first lists already added
in v0.8.20. Retain the actual five-arm list as a distinct execution cohort.

Add versioned advisory features, not immediate hard filters:

- Stock strength relative to benchmark and its industry/sector proxy.
- Group participation and leadership persistence, with missing theme/catalyst
  evidence distinguished from an actual weak reading.
- Contraction measured relative to ADR/ATR, base maturity, EMA slope and distance
  to a usable trigger; test the mirrored rating framework only after Phase 0.
- Fundamental growth/catalyst context only with source and availability dates.

Do not use today's leaders as yesterday's universe. Do not penalize absent
fundamental data as if it were negative growth. Changing a ranking must not
silently change the candidate pool too; report those as separate experiments.

Deliverable: paired selection report showing trigger frequency, confirmed signals,
executable signals, costs, concentration and subsequent economic outcomes.

**Exit gate:** a challenger offers measurable incremental executable opportunity,
not just more touches. If economic evidence is insufficient, continue collecting;
do not promote based on the current three-session retrospective comparison.

## Phase 3 — implement the missing entry models in shadow research

**Question:** Are we waiting for daily breakouts while the method would enter a
lower, clearly defined reversal in a leading stock?

Build independently selectable, versioned state machines:

1. **Undercut/reclaim:** freeze a known support/EMA/pivot, observe an actual break
   beneath it, then a reclaim. Specify depth, validity horizon, confirmation,
   repeat-attempt limits and failure/invalidation rules before testing. The level
   must be known before the undercut. A gap or missing interval is not evidence
   that the complete sequence occurred.
2. **30-minute pivot:** freeze the qualifying daily/weekly support zone, observe a
   pullback, identify the first qualifying completed green 30-minute candle, then
   require a subsequent break of its high. Do not buy using the completed
   candle's high before that high was knowable.
3. **Breakout control:** retain the current breakout path. Compare 5-minute and
   15-minute confirmation on identical candidates. A 30-minute pivot setup is not
   equivalent to setting a generic breakout's timeframe to 30 minutes.

For each model specify the market context, stop anchor, fresh evidence required,
volume interpretation and target handling. “Enter as the level breaks” differs
from our closed-bar entry invariant: initially use a documented closed-bar
experimental interpretation. Intrabar execution would require a separate platform
design and approval, not a hidden exception in a Cartel setup.

Preserve day-low-so-far versus breakout-candle/pivot-low as separate stop variants.
Do not select the tighter stop after seeing which one survived. Model overnight
gaps, halted feeds, restart, stale corrections, duplicate bars, partial evidence,
failed breaks and subsequent valid attempts. A repeat entry requires a new valid
signal and current admission; it must not resurrect a consumed signal.

Deliverables: source-linked specifications, pure evaluators, frozen source-example
fixtures, causal replay, shadow UI and live/replay parity tests. Report missed
opportunities and false positives, including trades the control avoided.

**Exit gate:** no look-ahead or duplicate entry, deterministic replay over the
same evidence, and model-specific economic comparison. Remain shadow-only until
the model and its interpretation receive an explicit activation verdict.

## Phase 4 — compare vehicle economics and realizable exits

**Question:** When does the stock thesis make sense but its option does not?

For the same eligible signal compare the reviewed option, ordinary unlevered
shares, and passing on the trade. Keep the cash cap and stress-risk cap visible;
report both risk-normalized and cash-normalized results, since the exposures are
not equivalent. No cheaper low-delta contract solely to make an order possible.

Option valuation uses actual or time-qualified bid/ask evidence, contract
multiplier, fees, liquidity, expiry and available exit observations. Stress wide
spreads, premium decay and overnight gaps. Distinguish conservative full-premium
loss from an estimate of loss at the underlying stop; do not manufacture that
estimate from stale Greeks. Missing observations prevent an economic verdict.

Validate whole-unit exits at one, two, three and larger contract quantities.
Document where our minimum affordable position cannot reproduce quarter trims.
Compare current allocations and source-supported alternatives with fixed entry
and initial risk. Retain meaningful resistance rather than skipping it to make
reward/risk attractive. Dynamic runners remain exposed to reversals and gaps.

Deliverables: vehicle decision table, quantity/exit feasibility report, paired
after-cost outcomes, and explicit pass decisions. No automatic shares fallback
or new allocation becomes active merely because its simulation looks better.

**Exit gate:** comparable cost evidence and realizable integer allocations; no
use of peak-trim return as weighted realized P&L. If neither vehicle is suitable,
passing is a valid outcome.

## Phase 5 — prospective Practice experiment and promotion

Freeze a baseline and one challenger at a time. Selection, entry, vehicle and
exit changes must first be measured independently; only then test a specified
combination. Match session/candidate cohorts and account for correlated trades.

Start in non-ordering shadow mode. A proposed first checkpoint is at least 20
trading sessions and 30 executable signals per compared variant, across more
than one market condition. These are minimum review points, not statistical
proof or an automatic pass. Sparse signals mean a longer observation period,
not manufactured trades or moving thresholds.

Predeclare primary metrics: net dollars and net expectancy after costs; secondary
metrics: drawdown, adverse/favorable excursion, exposure, turnover, fill rate,
holding time, concentration and data/quote coverage. Report uncertainty with
session-clustered comparisons and sensitivity to worse fills/costs. Preserve a
held-out period; do not repeatedly tune on it. A result driven by one name/day
does not establish general improvement.

Promotion requires an explicit review that covers:

- No known look-ahead, attribution, quote, coverage or lifecycle defect.
- Benefit persists under plausible transaction-cost stress and is not explained
  only by higher exposure or a different opportunity denominator.
- Drawdown and concentration fit predeclared tolerances; the comparison has enough
  complete evidence to distinguish benefit from noise.
- A limited Practice activation plan, rollback setting, ownership, and preserved
  control records. Resolve economic tolerances in the experiment specification
  before outcomes are inspected. Inconclusive results do not pass.

For an order-capable Practice trial, avoid two real orders for the same experiment
signal in the same book. Use non-ordering matched controls or a separately approved
sim cohort with explicit attribution and account caps. Keep technique book routing
and risk invariants; no shared-book fallback. Protective exits remain active on
rollback. Live activation is outside this plan.

## Phase 6 — release, operating review and documentation

Release small reviewable changes with the source matrix, tests, result artifacts
and migration/rollback notes. Resolve version collisions against current main and
the actual runtime. Deploy only through the guarded deployment path outside prime
hours; verify exact build, artifact receipt, quiescence release, restored arms,
managed positions and resting orders. Compare other teams' settings before/after.

Update METHOD, SOURCES, TRADING-RULES, PROFITABILITY-RESEARCH, DELIVERY-STATUS and
user-visible explanations as applicable. Update PLATFORM-RULES only for shared
behavior. Keep source guidance distinct from experimental numerical choices.

Daily review records actual P&L and the opportunity funnel. Weekly review decides
whether to continue, reject, or propose the next experiment. Do not tune rules
daily in reaction to that day's winners. Stop development work that merely adds
complexity without improving fidelity, evidence quality or a testable hypothesis.

frozen inputs, acceptance tests, result limits, runtime scope and rollback. Prefer
one consolidated review per packet rather than many partial review cycles.
