I've used this simple strategy on $SPY & $QQQ to become a full time trader 🧵

Bookmark and study it and you could do the same👇
Start by charting out the Previous Day High and Low zones. Very simple 📝

Previous day high zone = High of day wick to the following candle body.

Previous day low zone = Low of day wick to the following candle body.

Here's a visual example of todays PDH & PDL zones 👇
I use these 2 major areas of support & resistance to guide me through the price action the following day.

Above the PDH zone = Favor calls 📈

Below the PDL zone = Favor puts 📉

Trading within the previous days range can often be a little more tricky. If you are not consistently profitable yet consider avoiding that area completely 👇
Follow the EMA trends!

Here’s the cheat sheet 👇

Bullish EMA trend… 📈

Above 200 EMA = Bullish
Above 200 + 48 EMA = More Bullish
Above 200 + 48 + 13 EMA = Mega Bullish

Bearish EMA trend… 📉

Below 200 EMA = Bearish
Below 200 + 48 EMA = More Bearish
Below 200 + 48 + 13 EMA = Mega Bearish

Life is easier when you trade the trend!

Study these simple charts of bullish / bearish trends.

So what happened today? How can we find a high quality trade using the zones & EMA's mentioned above?

Well, for starters let's just focus on the zones.

Not only do they give you a game plan for where you want to look for a trade... they will also show you areas you want to avoid.

Bounced off the PDL support = Bullish
Broke the PDH resistance = Bullish
PDH flipped to support on the retest = Bullish

There were no short / put trades to be had until the supply zone target was reached 👇
Now let's bring in the EMA trend to assist us even further!

What are we looking at? Well... A bullish EMA trend 📈

By combining the support & resistance zones with the EMA trends you start to get a pretty good idea of which direction has the better R/R 👇
Now let's zoom in and take a look at this trade using just the support & resistance zones,

Wait patiently for the PDH to break then keep the focus on calls up to our next resistance zone 👇
Let's add those EMA's in again! Now we had multiple confirmations to trade the upside toda. So thats exacly what we did 👇
The best part is that it completely frees up your bias which enables you to simply trade the chart!

This setup works equally as good both long or short👇
Now... I'm not saying this is the only setup to trade!

But if you are not yet profitable, you need to stick to 1 or 2 setups religiously!

Learning patience and discipline is a must! And in order to do that you need a high quality setup that you are going to wait for!

No more scalping every other candle ❌
No more chasing every move ❌
No more 14 trades a day ❌

Just 1 or 2 high quality trades and a strong mindset that allows you to wait for them! ✅
