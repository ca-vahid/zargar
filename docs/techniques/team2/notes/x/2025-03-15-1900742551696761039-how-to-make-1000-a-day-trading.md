How to make $1000 a day trading $SPY & $QQQ 🧵

This is the mega thread you will be the only one you need...

I follow this exact strategy everyday to find trades like these week after week! 👇

#1 = The Previous Day High & Low zones!

Let's start with how to draw them, it's very simple...

(15 minute chart)

Previous Day High Zone = High of day wick to the following candle body ✅

Previous Day Low Zone = Low of day wick to the following candle body ✅

Visual example 👇
How to utilize these zones...

These are the main areas of support & resistance I watch the following day.

This gives me 4 scenarios to watch for ⬇️

1️⃣ Break PDH = Focus on calls 📈
2️⃣ Reject PDH = Focus on puts 📉
3️⃣ Bounce PDL = Focus on calls 📈
4️⃣ Break PDL = Focus on puts 📉

*Note, I prefer to size down for scenarios 2 & 3 (Range Day) and size up for scenarios 1 & 4 (Expansion Day)

Visual example 👇
#2 = Follow the EMA trends!

Trading with the EMA trends makes it much easier to be on the right side of the move...

Here's the EMA's I use ⬇️

(2 minute chart)

13 EMA 🟡
48 EMA 🟣
200 EMA 🔴

Let's take a look at how to utilize these 👇
Bullish EMA trend 📈

13 EMA on top 🟡
48 EMA in middle 🟣
200 EMA on bottom 🔴

Think of it like this ⬇️

Above 200 EMA = Bullish
Above 200 + 48 EMA = More Bullish
Above 200 + 48 + 13 EMA = Mega Bullish

Visual example 👇
Bearish EMA trend 📉

200 EMA on top 🔴
48 EMA in middle 🟣
13 EMA on bottom 🟡

Reverse the thought process from above ⬇️

Under 200 EMA = Bearish
Under 200 + 48 EMA = More Bearish
Under 200 + 48 + 13 EMA = Mega Bearish

Visual example 👇
#3 = Trade the flags!

Bull / Bear Flags are one of the most reliable chart patterns out there. I add them to my system for added confirmation.

The main focus is...

Bull Flags above the Previous Day High 📈
Bear Flags below the Previous Day Low 📉

So basically I am focusing on flags that align with the EMA trends and the 4 scenarios provided above.

Visual example 👇

You could trade any of these 3 techniques by themselves and probably find some decent success...

But letting 2 or 3 them come together into 1 trade gives you multiple confirmations and increases your win rate substantially!

Let's look at some examples of this 👇

Breaks Previous Day Low Support ✅

Bonus tip...

Avoid trading areas on the chart where these EMA's get tight and bunched up ❌

There is usually no momentum in those areas creating choppy price action that causes many unnecessary losses!

Visual example of the difference 👇
Bonus tip...

Mark the Pre Market High & Low (4am - 9:30am EST) everyday before open.

These are great additional levels to watch for possible entry points into trades.

(Try to avoid trading inside of that Pre Market High & Low range) That is often an area where price tends to be choppy!

* I do make certain exceptions to this rule if I really like the setup. But newer traders should avoid it completely.
