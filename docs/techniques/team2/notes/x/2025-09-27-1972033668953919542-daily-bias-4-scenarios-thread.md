$SPY ~ This is one of the most powerful strategies around for determining your daily bias while day trading...

Let me teach you how to use it right now 🧵
Start by marking the Previous Day High & Low Zones before the market opens...

I use the 15 minute chart and make a small zone from high / low of day wick to the following candle body.

This gives me 4 scenarios to watch that day 👇
Scenario #1 ~ Price breaks above the Previous Day High Resistance.

This is the most Bullish scenario. When this happens I keep my focus on calls / upside 📈

I look for confirmations of strength with things like EMA trends and Bull Flags 👇
Scenario #1 is when I am really leaning into these EMA dips and Bull Flags to take advantage of the strong uptrend 👇
Scenario #2 ~ Price rejects the Previous Day High Resistance.

This is a Bearish scenario. When this happens I am focusing on puts / downside to PDL 📉
Scenario #2 is technically setting up a "range day" and I trade them a bit more cautiously.

I typically look for confirmation following the PDH rejection with things like Bearish EMA crosses , PML breaks, bear flags etc... 👇
Scenario #3 ~ Price bounces off the Previous Day Low Support.

This is a Bullish scenario. When this happens I am focusing on calls / upside to the PDH 📈
Just like scenario #2, scenario #3 is also technically setting up a "range day" so I trade them a bit more cautiously.

I like to confirm the support bounce with things like EMA trends, Bull Flags, Pre Market levels etc... 👇
Scenario #4 ~ Price breaks below the Previous Day Low Support.

This is the most Bearish scenario. When this happens I am keeping my focus on puts / downside until my targets are hit.

I am looking for confirmations of weakness with things like EMA trends and Bear Flags 👇
Scenario #4 is when I am really leaning into these Bear Flags and EMA pullbacks to take advantage of the strong downtrend 👇
The directional bias of nearly every trade I take is influenced by the reaction to these Previous Day High / Low Zones.

This is just one of many tips and tricks I use daily as a professional day trader. Hope you find it helpful 💚
