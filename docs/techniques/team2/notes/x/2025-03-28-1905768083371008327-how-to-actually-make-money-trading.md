How to actually make money trading $SPY & $QQQ 🧵

Doesn't matter if the market is going up or down, this is the only strategy you will need 👇

Take notes because this will be worth more than any expensive trading course you could ever buy 📝

The first thing you want to do is chart out the previous day high & low zones.

Here's how you do that... (Use the 15 minute chart)

Previous Day High Zone = High of day wick to the following candle body ✅

Previous Day Low Zone = Low of day wick to the following candle body ✅

Below is a visual example of how to do this 👇
Now lets break down how to use these zones...

These are the main areas of support & resistance to watch the following day!

This gives you 4 of the strongest scenarios to watch.

1️⃣ Break PDH = Favor calls 📈
2️⃣ Reject PDH = Favor puts 📉
3️⃣ Bounce PDL = Favor calls 📈
4️⃣ Break PDL = Favor puts 📉

This is the cheat code for determining daily direction!

Here's a visual example of this 👇
Next you want to focus on the EMA trends...

Trading with the EMA trends makes your job as a trader so much easier!

After years of studying these are the ones I use ⬇️

(2 minute chart)

13 EMA 🟡
48 EMA 🟣. .
200 EMA 🔴

Let's break down how to utilize these 👇
1️⃣ The bullish EMA trend...

13 EMA on top 🟡
48 EMA in middle 🟣
200 EMA on bottom 🔴

Here's a simple cheat sheet to remember,

Above 200 EMA = Bullish
Above 200 + 48 EMA = More Bullish
Above 200 + 48 + 13 EMA = Mega Bullish

Visual example of a bullish EMA trend 👇
2️⃣ The bearish EMA trend...

200 EMA on top 🔴
48 EMA in middle 🟣
13 EMA on bottom 🟡

Reverse the cheat sheet from above,

Under 200 EMA = Bearish
Under 200 + 48 EMA = More Bearish
Under 200 + 48 + 13 EMA = Mega Bearish

Visual example of a bearish EMA trend 👇
3️⃣ No EMA trend...

The best part about EMA trends is they will also show you the areas not to trade!

Tight bunched up EMA's = No momentum ❌

These are the choppy areas where it becomes easy to take losses because neither Bulls or Bears have control of the price action.

It's balanced and literally a lose lose situation, avoid those areas 👇
Now you have 2 of the best confirmations in the game!

1️⃣ The Previous Day High & Low Zones ✅
2️⃣ The Bullish & Bearish EMA trends ✅

Those alone will have you on the right side of the move day after day! But lets add in a bonus confirmation that I love to watch for...

Bull / Bear Flags! Its very simple,

Focus on Bull Flags that align with Bullish EMA trends

Focus on Bear Flags that align with Bearish EMA trends

These flag patterns are the most simple and effective chart pattens out there. They show up day after day and are a great tool for added confirmation and entries.
