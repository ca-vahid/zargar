# The "EMA fan" (Oct 2025)

$SPY • The "EMA fan" is one of my favorite strategies for avoiding chop and trading momentum 📝

It's very simple. Here's how it works…

Add the 13 • 48 • 200 EMA's onto the 2 minute chart.

When those 3 EMA's are tightly stacked or "braided together" it's a clear indication of chop and lack of momentum ❌

Now, when those 3 EMA's start to space out and drift apart it's a clear indication of trend and momentum coming in ✅

Avoid the chop. Trade the trend 🤙
