$SPY This is one of the best ways to form your directional bias each day…

Start by marking these 4 areas ✍️

Previous Day High ✔️
Previous Day Low ✔️
Pre Market High ✔️
Pre Market Low ✔️

If price is above the Previous Day High + Pre Market High = Favor calls 📈

If price is below the Previous Day Low + Pre Market Low = Favor puts 📉
