# My ultimate guide for reading price action: 3 techniques (Mar 2026)

This is my ultimate guide for reading price action 🧵

After nearly a decade of trading... These are the top 3 techniques I continue to use day after day!

$SPY $QQQ $IWM 👇

---

#1 - Mark these 4 levels every morning ✍️

Previous Day High (PDH)
Previous Day Low (PDL)
Pre Market High (PMH)
Pre Market Low (PML)

These are key areas of support & resistance to watch

---

*Note... I add a zone off those PDH & PDL levels to give me a small area to watch for bounces & rejections 👇

---

The idea behind the PDH & PDL zones are very simple.

I watch for these 4 scenarios 👀

Break PDH = Favor calls
Reject PDH = Favor puts
Bounce PDL = Favor calls
Break PDL = Favor puts

This is my first guide for directional bias👇

---

The Pre Market High & Low is my secondary range to watch.

Mainly used on inside days or as confirmation levels if they fall outside the previous days range 👇

---

#3 - 15 minute candle closes.

This is one of my favorite ways to confirm level breaks.

A majority of intraday liquidity sweeps occur on the 15 minute chart. So letting that 15 minute candle body close can be very helpful to avoid fake outs 👇
