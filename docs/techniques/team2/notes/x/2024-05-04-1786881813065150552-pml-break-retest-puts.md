# Pre-market low break & retest (puts) (May 2024)

$SPY Pro Trading Tip 📝

This is an awesome setup that is both simple & effective for playing the downside (puts/shorts).

The best part is you don't have to be a highly experienced trader to understand and apply it!

It's called the pre market low break & retest. Here's how it works ⬇️

1 • Mark the lowest price on the chart from 4am - 9:30am ET.

2 • Wait for price to break under that level if the market is looking weak.

3 • Watch for a retest/rejection of that pre market low level to enter puts.

4 • Keep a stop loss just above that PML level for a low risk / high reward trade opportunity.
