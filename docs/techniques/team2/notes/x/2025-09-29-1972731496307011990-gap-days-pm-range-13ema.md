# Gap up / down days: PM range 15m close, then the first 13 EMA dip (Sep 2025)

Here's an awesome trading tip for gap up / down days 📝

The first thing I like to focus on for direction is the Pre Market High / Low range.

I look for a 15 minute candle close outside the Pre Market range, then watch the dips to the 13 EMA on the 2 minute chart.

Often times that first 13ema pullback is a money printer! Keep an eye on it 👀

Add a clean Bull Flag in a Bullish EMA trend… oh my, A+ setup 👇
