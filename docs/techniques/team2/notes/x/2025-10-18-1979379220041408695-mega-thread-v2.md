Step 1 - Mark your support & resistance zones!

I start by marking the Previous Day High & Low zones before the market opens...

I use the 15 minute chart and make a small zone from high / low of day wick to the following candle body.

Lets break it down why i do this 👇

---

These Previous Day High / Low Zones give me 4 scenarios to watch that will help determine my bias for the day👀

These are the 4 most common scenarios that play out on a day to day basis ⬇️

1️⃣ Break PDH = Focus on calls 📈
2️⃣ Reject PDH = Focus on puts 📉
3️⃣ Bounce PDL = Focus on calls 📈
4️⃣ Breaks PDL = Focus on puts 📉

Study this chart 👇

---

Example of Scenario #1 ~ Price breaks above the Previous Day High Resistance Zone.

This is a Bullish scenario 📈

When this happens I am keeping my focus on calls / upside as long we hold above that PDH zone 👇

---

Example of Scenario #2 ~ Price rejects the Previous Day High Resistance Zone.

This is a Bearish scenario📉

When this happens I keep my focus on puts / downside and typically target a move back to the Previous Day Low support zone 👇

---

Example of Scenario #3 ~ Price bounces off the Previous Day Low Support Zone.

This is a Bullish scenario 📈

When this happens I am keeping my focus on calls / upside and typically targeting a move back up to the Previous Day High resistance zone 👇

---

Example of Scenario #4 ~ Price breaks below the Previous Day Low Support Zone.

This is a Bearish scenario 📉

When this happens I keep my focus on puts / downside as long as price holds under that PDL support zone 👇

Step 2 ~ Add these 3 EMA's to your chart!

13 EMA🟡
48 EMA🟣
200 EMA🔴

Let's break down exactly how to utilize these EMA's👇

---

Here's the cheat sheet I follow 📝

(2 minute chart for trading shorter term trends. Higher timeframes for a wider outlook)

Bullish EMA trend 📈

13 EMA on top 🟡
48 EMA in middle 🟣
200 EMA on bottom 🔴

Remember,

Above 200 EMA = Bullish
Above 200 + 48 EMA = More Bullish
Above 200 + 48 + 13 EMA = Mega Bullish

---

Bearish EMA trend 📉

200 EMA on top 🔴
48 EMA in middle 🟣
13 EMA on bottom 🟡

Remember,

Below 200 EMA = Bearish
Below 200 + 48 EMA = More Bearish
Below 200 + 48 + 13 EMA = Mega Bearish

---

Pay close attention to the "EMA Fan" while these trends start to form.

Avoid trading the areas where these 3 EMA's are tightly spaced and braided together. That is a clear sign of chop or lack of momentum.

When those 3 EMA's start to space out and drift apart it's a good sign that momentum is coming in and the trend is starting to pick up strength.

This is a game changing technique for avoiding choppy areas 👇
