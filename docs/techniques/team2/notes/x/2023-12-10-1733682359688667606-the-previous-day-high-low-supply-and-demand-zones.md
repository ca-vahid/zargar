The previous day high / low Supply and Demand zones ⚡️

These bad boys are money! I’ve been preaching them for years and we bank daily on them💰

Mark these zones every night and use them to help guide you the following day.

Here’s a quick chart review showing a few key things to look for when trading them ⬇️

• Watch for support at PDL and resistance at PDH.

• Watch for the break & retest of either! Favor shorts/puts under PDL and long/calls over PDH.

• Choppier conditions usually occur inside of the previous days range, while smoother price action usually occurs outside of it.
