# Favorite setup for trading all-time highs on SPY: PMH break, retest, stop on 2m close (Apr 2026)

My favorite setup for trading all time highs on $SPY 📝

Step 1 • Mark the Pre Market High (4am to 9:30am est)

Step 2 • Wait for price to break above that PMH level (15 minute candle close)

Step 3 • Enter calls on retest of Pre Market High (2 minute strategy)

*Stop loss is a 2 minute candle close under that PMH level.

For most people trading calls at all time highs is "scary". Having a R/R setup this good will eliminate that fear.
