# Sunday preparation thread (Sep 2025)

$SPY Sunday preparation thread 🧵

These are some of the key technicals I will be focusing on to set myself up for another profitable week of trading.

Let's break down a few of them 👇

---

The 13 / 48 / 200 EMA fan ✅

This one is really helpful for trend traders who prefer buying dips in those areas with one sided momentum.

Study the EMA fan! Avoid that chop and watch for those dip buys 👇

---

Entering near my stop levels ✅

Whether it's an EMA pullback or a key level break & retest. Taking your entry as close to that as possible keeps losses small and makes them a lot easier to recover from on the next trade.

Combining this with the EMA fan above gives us some lower risk high / high reward entry points 👇

---

Sizing correctly for my edge ✅

I have always preferred trading expansion over inside days. So I am a bit more cautious when price is within the previous days range.

And many times, I will choose to avoid trading the pre market range all together.

This is a loose guide on my sizing technique👇

---

Expansion vs inside days ✅

These are the 4 most common scenarios to watch for on a daily basis.

This is a big part of my directional bias everyday 👇

---

Multiple timeframe confluence ✅

15 minute = Levels & Zones 👀
5 minute = Flags / trend lines ✍️
2 minute = EMA trends & entries 🎯

You don't need to be glued to all timeframes. But a quick glance at them throughout the day can really help give you a better outlook on when those stars are aligning for a great trade 👇

---

Positive attitude / mindset ✅

Remember... A positive attitude leads to positive outcomes 💚

Enjoy the rest of your Sunday everyone. Have a great week of trading 🤙
