# My EMA trend system (Feb 2026)

$SPY • My EMA trend system is a major part of my trading strategy!

It will allow you to capture momentum and trade on the right side of the move day after day.

Here's how I use it…

Add the 13 • 48 • 200 EMA's onto the 2 minute chart (ext hours on)

• Bullish EMA trend 📈

13 EMA top 🟡
48 EMA middle 🟣
200 EMA bottom 🔴

Always favor calls in a bullish trend.

• Bearish EMA trend 📉

200 EMA top 🔴
48 EMA middle 🟣
13 EMA bottom 🟡

Always favor puts in a bearish trend.

Now… This part is important👇

When those 3 EMA's are tightly stacked or "braided together" it's a clear indication of chop and lack of momentum ❌

When those 3 EMA's start to space out and drift apart it's a clear indication of trend and momentum coming in ✅

Aim to avoid the areas of consolidation and only trade while price is in either a Bullish or Bearish trend 🤙
