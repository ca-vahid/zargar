How to make $1000 a day trading $SPY 🧵

Yes, I actually do this every day!

Here's my strategy 👇
Let me kick this off by saying that trading is hard! It took me years to achieve any sort of consistency with it.

There's a million ways to make a million $! This is mine.

Let's dive in! 💚
Start by marking the Previous Days High & Low.

Every morning before the market opens I make a small "zone" using the previous days high and low. I connect that to a nearby candle body.

This gives me my main support & resistance zone to watch the following day 👇
Why is this so important?

Because it gives you a clear plan of attack for the following day! Here's the breakdown ⬇️

For trend trading...

Above the PDH zone = Favor calls 📈
Below the PDL zone = Favor puts 📉

For range trading...

Reject the PDH zone = Favor puts 📉
Bounce at PDL zone = Favor calls 📈
So what exactly happened the following day and where were the opportunities using the guide above?

Well... we got a textbook break & retest of the Previous Day High Resistance Zone to get long with calls!

Now let's dive into some further confirmations to gain even more confidence in a trade like this 👇
Zoom in a little closer on that PDH retest above and what do we see? One of my all time favorite chart patterns!!

The "Bull Flag"

Get familiar with this one! Its simple, reliable, and shows up all the time to add even more confidence to trades and trends 👇

The next thing I am focusing is trading on the right side of the EMA trends!

Here's a quick rundown, very simple ⬇️

📈 Bullish EMA trend...

🟡 13 EMA on top
🟣 48 EMA in middle
🔴 200 EMA on bottom

📉 Bearish EMA trend...

🔴 200 EMA on top
🟣 48 EMA in middle
🟡 13 EMA on bottom

It's a great cheat sheet for trend trading 👇
Now add all this together and what do we get?

A killer A+ setup with multiple confirmations!

Break & retest of PDH = Bullish ✅
Bull Flag Breakout = Bullish ✅
Bullish EMA trend = Bullish ✅

This was an easy 150%+ trade checking all the boxes!
