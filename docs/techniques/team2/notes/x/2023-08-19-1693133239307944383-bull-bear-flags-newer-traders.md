🚨 Newer traders!!🚨

This is one of the most simple and effective chart patterns to learn & study 📖

The “Bull” & “Bear” Flag 🏳️

Learning to spot a chart that is “flagging” can be a game changer for your trading! (1/3) 👇

$SPY $QQQ $TSLA $IWM $AMD
The “Bull” Flag! 🐂

After a strong initial move up watch the pullback! This is where a potential bull flag will be setting up.

Newer traders will often go short here trying to time the top and fail to see the flag building as price consolidates 👇 (2/3)
The “Bear” Flag! 🐻

After a strong initial move down, watch the pullback! This is where a potential bear flag will be setting up.

Newer traders will often go long here as they attempt to time the bottom and fail to see the flag building as price consolidates 👇 (3/3)
