Here's the simple cheat sheet for Bullish vs Bearish EMA trends.

Bullish EMA trend 📈

13 EMA on top 🟡
48 EMA in middle 🟣
200 EMA on bottim 🔴

These EMA's will be your ares of support in an uptrend. (Dip to get long)

Bearish EMA trend 📉

200 EMA on top 🔴
48 EMA in middle 🟣
13 EMA on bottom 🟡

These EMA's will be your areas of resistance in a downtrend. (Pullbacks to get short)
