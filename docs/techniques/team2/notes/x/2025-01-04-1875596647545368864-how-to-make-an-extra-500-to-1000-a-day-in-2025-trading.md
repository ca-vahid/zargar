How to make an extra $500 to $1000 a day in 2025 trading $SPY $QQQ $IWM 🧵

These tips and techniques I'm about to show you are the foundation of my trading strategy 💚
First off! Don't let the catchy title of this thread fool you. Trading is extremely hard and took me years to become consistently profitable!

I try to help shorten that journey for others by showing what works for me. Not make it appear "easy"!

With that say... lets dive in 👇
Start by marking these 4 levels each day...

Previous Day High (PDH) ✅
Previous Day Low (PDL) ✅
Pre Market High (PMH) ✅
Pre Market Low (PML) ✅

Here is an example of where to find these levels. This was $QQQ yesterday ⬇️
The idea behind these 4 levels is this...

Above PDH = Bullish
Above PDH + PMH = Mega Bullish

Below PDL = Bearish
Below PDL + PML = Mega Bearish

Between PDH & PDL = Range (chop)
Between PMH & PML = Tight range (Mega Chop)

It's just a cheat sheet to follow.
Now if we take that basic cheat sheet and apply in to the $QQQ chart above we can see exactly why I focused on taking calls where I did yesterday...

I waited for the "Mega Bullish" indicator to fire (Above PDH + PMH) and focused on calls to join the trend.

This is a simple & effective way to identify uptrends, downtrends, and areas of chop!
Second part of my system is utilizing EMA trends!

I use the 13 / 48 / 200 EMA's on the 2 minute chart.

Here's my cheat sheet for these ⬇️

Under 200 EMA = Bearish
Under 200 + 48 = More Bearish
Under 200 + 48 + 13 EMA = Mega Bearish

Simply flip it around for longs 🔄
Now circle back to our same $QQQ chart and add in this secondary form of confirmation!

By combining the levels + EMA's you are rarely caught fighting the trend. Trading with the trend makes life a lot easier because momentum is on your side.
Final touch on this system is adding in some basic chart patterns to the mix!

Bull / Bear Flags are one of my favorites. They are easy to spot and highly effective.

Here's my cheat sheet for flags ⬇️

👀 Bear Flags under the PDL
👀 Bull Flags over the PDH

After years of trial and error I've found that combining these 3 elements gives me plenty of opportunities to make a few bucks while keeping things simple!
