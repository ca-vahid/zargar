# How to find key support & resistance levels/zones (Sep 2023)

$SPY How to find key support & resistance levels/zones! 🧵

I'm about to walk you through my simple process for finding major support & resistance areas to trade off of everyday!

These are quick & easy to chart and the effectiveness of them is hard to beat! Lets take a look ⬇️

---

Going to keep this short and sweet by highlighting the 4 major areas i'm looking at each day along with charts & examples of how we trade them!

Whether you are an expert trader or just starting out and never charted a level before, these are the areas you should be watching! ⬇️

---

The first 2 areas i'm watching each day are the previous day high and the previous day low. This is the easiest way to get sense of direction for the day.

Price over the PDH = Favor calls 📈
Price under the PDL = Favor puts 📉
Between PDL & PDH = Balanced. Cautious of chop 📉📈

---

When price breaks under the previous days support it tells you that sellers are likely taking control of the day. When this happens the better R/R trades are usually going to be to the downside📉

First example here is $IWM last week. Note the selling pressure on the zone break⬇️

---

Another example here on $TSLA

After some back and forth between buyers & sellers within the PDH and PDL range we break the PDL support zone.

This is where it becomes a one sided fight and sellers where able to take control. From there we are looking to trade puts on pullbacks⬇️

---

The same way we watch the PDL for a breakdown 📉 We watch the PDH zone for a breakout 📈

When price breaks above the previous days resistance it tell you that buyers are taking control for the day. We want to focus on buying calls until the next resistance is hit ⬇️

---

We can see from this example on $IWM how quickly price can move when a major resistance zone is cracked.

Having these zones charted enables you to be ready and capitalize on these moves up ⬇️

---

This brings us to the second major areas of support & resistance i'm watching each day!

The Pre Market High & Pre Market Low 🗒️

I mark these 2 levels every day before market opens. They are excellent areas to watch for gauging price direction ⬇️

---

This gives you a second range to watch for expansion. If price is stuck between the PML and PMH watch for choppier conditions.

Use the these like any other support and resistance levels. Look for bounces and rejections, and break and retest entries ⬇️

---

Here's a perfect example on $QQQ last week. Price chops around between the pre market low and pre market high before eventually breaking out of range and giving a perfect break and retest entry.

These 2 levels alone set us up to avoid the chop & catch this explosive move ⬇️

---

Another textbook example on $TSLA of the pre market high break and retest ⬇️

---

One of the best ways to scan for a strong stock is to look for a break of both the previous day resistance and the pre market high!

This tells you that buyers are in control. Never try to short stocks that are breaking resistance levels! Its much easier to join the trend ⬇️

---

We can see how often these 4 levels tell the story for the day.

Here we are simply ranging between the pre market high and previous day high/low zones. Having these simple levels and zones charted gives you a crystal clear picture of what is happening ⬇️

The examples of price respecting these 4 major areas of support and resistance are endless! I encourage everyone to give them a try and see if this is something you can add to your system to improve your trading 🤙
