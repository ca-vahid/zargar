# EMA system for entries and trend strength: 13 / 48 / 200 as lines of defense (Apr 2025)

$SPY $QQQ Here's a helpful EMA system for finding entries and gauging trend strength!

2 minute chart , 13 • 48 • 200 EMA's

🟡 13 EMA • First line of defense. These are the pullbacks I'm looking at when the trend is strong. This is also the first dip I'm looking to buy after a major support / resistance break!

🟣 48 EMA • Second line of defense. Strong trends usually take a deeper pullback at some point. This is where I'm watching that 48 EMA to hold. After a few 13 EMA taps, I'll start watching for that possible 48 EMA dip.

🔴 200 EMA • Line in the sand. The 200 EMA is often a strong support / resistance level. I generally like to trade calls when price is over it, and puts when price is under it.

This is just a quick overview of how they can help assist you with trading the trend 🤙
