The blueprint to start making $1k/day trading $SPY 🧵
Support & Resistance...

It's by far the most effective tool for trading the stock market! If you are newer to support & resistance, start with this simple technique👇

Make a zone using the Previous Day High & Low.

Previous Day High = Resistance. Focus on calls/upside if price breaks above it.

Previous Day Low = Support. Focus on puts/downside if price breaks below it.

You can also trade inside the range using rejections/ bounces of those same areas.

Here's a visual example from yesterday ⬇️
EMA trends...

I use the 13 / 48 / 200 EMA's on the 2 minute chart.

Here's my quick cheat sheet 👇

Above 200 EMA = Bullish
Above 200 + 48 EMA = More Bullish
Above 200 + 48 + 13 EMA = Most Bullish

Below 200 EMA = Bearish
Below 200 + 48 EMA = More Bearish
Below 200 + 48 + 13 EMA = Most Bearish

Let me break down some visual examples because these EMA trends are so good! ⬇️
Bullish EMA trend 📈

Remember the cheat sheet...

200 EMA on bottom 🔴
48 EMA in middle 🟣
13 EMA on top 🟡

The deeper pullbacks will be towards the 200 and 48 while the most momentum will be happening when price is above all 3.

Save this chart for an easy visual 👇
Bearish EMA trend 📉

200 EMA on top 🔴
48 EMA in middle 🟣
13 EMA on bottom 🟡

Think of these as your lines of defense in a trend. They are moving support / resistance levels that price follows. So you want to be trading with them, not agains them!

Save the bearish EMA chart for an easy visual 👇
Bull / Bear Flags... My favorite chart pattern!

Here's my simple way to use them 👇

I look for Bull Flags when price is above my PDH zone and in a Bullish EMA trend 📈

I look for Bear Flags when price is below my PDL zone and in a Bearish EMA trend 📉

These are multiple confirmation trades. This means you have more than 1 single confirmation that you are on the right side of the move!

Where to enter?

Once you feel you have identified the trend, the best entries are going to be on pullbacks to the EMA's, key levels, or both.

The goal is to use the EMA trends to find low risk, high reward entries into the move. Here's an example of an entry technique I use nearly every day, Its the "risk 1 , win multiple" method.

The idea is to enter on a pullback to an EMA and keep your stop on the other side of it.

Here's a visual example 👇
Using this "risk 1 win multiple" technique can give you multiple opportunities to enter a trend.

This is also a very helpful way to hold your winning trades longer when using the 13 EMA break as a visual for a trailing stop on runners👇
This entry technique is just a simple way to ensure that you are keeping losers smaller than winners. Note the different between the loss and the win here.

The loss is restricted to 1 candle, while the win is able to run for multiple 👇

Where to sell?

Focus on base hits to start! My first target after entry is always that push to new highs / lows. Then runners are held for further gains.

Nothing will grow an account faster than locking in consistent wins while keeping losses small.

Home runs are fun and you WILL hit them! But they are not needed.
