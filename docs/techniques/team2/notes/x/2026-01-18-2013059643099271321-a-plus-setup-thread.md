# One of my favorite A+ setups: identify key levels, confirm the break, enter the trend (Jan 2026)

$SPY I'm about to teach you one of my favorite A+ setups to trade 🧵

By the end of this thread you will understand how to...

Identify key levels ✅
Confirm the break ✅
Enter the trend ✅

Lets dive right in so you can be executing A+ trades like this one every single week👇

---

Identifying key levels 📝

For this setup we are focusing on these 4 levels

Previous Day High ✔️
Previous Day Low ✔️
Pre Market High ✔️
Pre Market Low ✔️

These are strong levels that price trades around every single day. So they provide plenty of opportunities every single week 👇

---

*Note , I like to make a zone using the previous day high & low levels. These will my main areas of support & resistance to watch.

The pre market high & low (4am to 9:30am set) are my secondary levels to watch.

---

Next we confirm the break ✅

We wait patiently for price to break one of these key levels by allowing a 15 minute candle to close!

Waiting for that 15 minute candle close will prevent a lot of fake breakouts that many traders struggle with.

Image 1 of our example below shows the 15 minute chart and the candle close we are looking for 👇

---

I spend a lot of time highlighting how important this is for confirming level breaks.

This can really help newer traders with patience and avoiding false breakouts 👇

---

Once the level breaks we enter the trend ✅

I prefer to do this on the 2 minute chart and enter on the first or second pullback into the EMA's.

This is the same trade example from above. now we are focusing on image 2 which highlights the pullback entries on the 2 minute chart.

So we have the 15 minute break, then the 2 minute pullback 👇

---

Just for reference... This is my personal EMA system that I have been using for years.

Its really helpful...

13 / 48 / 200 EMA's on the 2 minute chart.

Bullish EMA trend 📈

13 EMA on top 🟡
48 EMA in middle 🟣
200 EMA on bottom 🔴

Bearish EMA trend 📉

200 EMA on top 🔴
48 EMA in middle 🟣
13 EMA on bottom 🟡

Now...

When those 3 EMA's are tightly stacked or braided together it's a clear indication of chop / lack of momentum.

When those 3 EMA's start to space out its a clear indication that momentum is coming in and the trend is developing.

For this particular setup I like to ensure this EMA trend system is aligning with my trades👇
