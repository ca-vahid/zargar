$SPY - A thread 🧵featuring the Previous Day High/Low Supply & Demand zone strategy! 🚨

This simple method is one of the most powerful and effective trading tools out there and i'm going to walk you through exactly how you can take advantage of it! Lets dive in and get to work👇
First you need to draw the zones! An easy general guide for creating them is this ⬇️

15 minute chart ✔️

High of day wick to the following candle body creates your supply zone.

Low of day wick to the following candle body creates your demand zone 👇
These areas are usually the major points where strong rejections or breakouts will happen.

They will tell you whether buyers or sellers are in control that day.

Break the PDH = Favor calls
Break the PDL = Favor puts
Break neither = Range day

Lets look at some ways to play them
As price hits these areas you want to watch them closely. There are 3 main ways to play them ⬇️

1- You can play the breakout of the zone ✔️
2- You can play the rejection of the zone ✔️
3- You can play the break & retest of the zone ✔️

I will go over an example of all 3 below 👇
1 - The breakout of a zone ⬇️

For this play you want to see sign of strong buyers at the supply zone or strong sellers at the the demand zone.

Remember, you are watching these zones for rejections or breakouts. When no sign of a rejection is present, get ready for a breakout 👇
2- The bounce/rejection of a zone ⬇️

Now on this play you are looking for the opposite. You want to see signs of strong sellers at supply or strong buyers at demand.

This implies that the zone will hold up and a rejection is likely 👇
3- The break & retest of a zone ⬇️

My personal favorite of the 3. It requires a little more patience to wait for the retest but its worth it.

Look for buyers to step in on retest of a supply zone and play calls.

Look for sellers on the retest of a demand zone and play puts 👇
You can add in some basic chart/candlestick patterns to help assist you with entries and confirmation as price reacts to these zones 👇
Bonus tip! You can also use them for price targets when already in a trade. This will help you avoid giving back profits on strong bounces/rejections at these zones 👇
