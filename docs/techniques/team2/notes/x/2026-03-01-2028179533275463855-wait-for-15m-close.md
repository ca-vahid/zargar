# Wait for the 15 minute candle close (Mar 2026)

$SPY • Here's a very helpful tip to help you avoid fakeouts and up your win rate! 📝

Wait for the 15 minute candle close ✅

A majority of liquidity sweeps occur on the 15 minute timeframe.

You can avoid a lot of those lower timeframe fakeouts by letting the 15 minute candle body close above / below your key level.

So rather than chasing the breakout, let it confirm on the 15, then buy the dips on the lower TF like the 2 and 5.
