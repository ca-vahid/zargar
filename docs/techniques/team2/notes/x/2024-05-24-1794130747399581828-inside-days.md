# Pro trading tip for inside days (May 2024)

Pro Trading Tip for Inside days 📝

We're going to use today's $QQQ chart for our example because I only review charts and trades that I personally alerted and traded. No hindsight trading, that's too easy.

• Inside day = Price trades within the previous days high and low range.

• This means we are right in the middle of our support and resistance zones. But after big days like yesterday where we had a massive range, we still have a lot of room to work with if we can determine the direction we will head.

• To help determine that direction, Mark the pre market high & low (Highest and lowest price from 4am - 9:30am eastern)

• Now wait for one of those levels to break. If we break PMH, favor longs up to resistance. If we break PML, favor puts down to support.

• About 30 minutes into today's session we broke the PMH and immediately went from chop to trend.

• As soon as price reached our resistance zone, we shifted back from trend to chop.

• This move up from pre market range break to previous days resistance usually the only clean move to catch on inside days.
