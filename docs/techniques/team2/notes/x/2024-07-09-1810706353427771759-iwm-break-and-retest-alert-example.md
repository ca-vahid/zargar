# IWM example: top-watch level, break, retest entry, stop above the level (Jul 2024)

Trading is hard! This is why I keep things as simple as possible. Not just for my own execution, but for anyone looking to learn and follow along.

Heres a good example of that on $IWM today 👇

"Top watch • Break under 201.84 and we are going to focus on puts today"

When it comes to levels, you can play the break or the retest. Often time I'm playing both.

When price retests that exact 201.84 level I give out my entry alert on which puts I just took. My stop loss is placed above that same 201.84 level.

If you think that's a good trade idea or like that r/r you can enter those same puts.

The previous day break and retest is a staple setup of ours. It's one you see me trade and review almost every single week ⬇️
