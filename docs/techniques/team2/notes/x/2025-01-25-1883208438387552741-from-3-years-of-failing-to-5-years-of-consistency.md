From 3+ years of failing... To 5+ years of consistency!

$SPY $QQQ $IWM

Here are 3 simple cheat sheets i follow 🧵
#1 - Mark these 4 levels and watch the reaction to them everyday ⬇️

Previous Day High (PDH) ✅
Previous Day Low (PDL) ✅
Pre Market High (PMH) ✅
Pre Market Low (PML) ✅

You will likely use these levels for the rest of your life afterwards!
Here's the cheat sheet for these 4 levels ⬇️

Above PDH = Bullish
Above PDH + PMH = Mega Bullish

Below PDL = Bearish
Below PDL + PML = Mega Bearish

Between PDH & PDL = Range (chop)
Between PMH & PML = Tight range (Mega Chop)

You can use this as a loose guide for daily bias!

#2 - Follow the EMA trends!

I use the 13 / 48 / 200 EMA's on the 2 minute chart.

Here's my EMA cheat sheet ⬇️

Under 200 EMA = Bearish
Under 200 + 48 = More Bearish
Under 200 + 48 + 13 EMA = Mega Bearish

Above 200 EMA = Bullish
Above 200 + 48 = More Bullish
Above 200 + 48 + 13 EMA = Mega Bullish

#3 - Bull / Bear flags ⬇️

Simple cheat sheet for flags...

Watch for Bull Flags above the PDH & PMH 👀

Watch for Bear Flags below the PDL & PML 👀

Here's an example of both 👇

If you are trading bull flags above the PDH with bullish EMA's you are winning much more than you are losing!

If you are trading bear flags under the PDL with bearish EMA's you are winning much more than you are losing!

These are 3 simple cheat sheets anyone can utilize and build on!

And remember! Trading is hard, keep it simple and have some fun. You are not going to get rich quick 💚
