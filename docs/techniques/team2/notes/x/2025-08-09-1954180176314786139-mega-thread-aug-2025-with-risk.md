Support & Resistance 📝

Start by marking these 4 levels every morning ✍️

Previous Day High ✅
Previous Day Low ✅
Pre Market High ✅
Pre Market Low ✅

These are key areas of support & resistance that get strong reactions for trade opportunities almost every single day.

Start with these 👇
Next i create a small "zone" using those Previous Day High & Low levels.

I use the 15 minute chart...

Mark the high of day wick to the following candle body for resistance zone.

Mark the low of day wick to the following candle body for support zone.

Something like this 👇
Now I have 4 main scenarios to determine my directional bias...

1️⃣ Break above PDH = favor calls 📈
2️⃣ Reject the PDH = favor puts 📉
3️⃣ Bounce at PDL = favor calls 📈
4️⃣ Break under PDL = favor puts 📉

These are the 4 main things I am looking for every single day 👇
My target zones above & below the Previous Day High / Low are the most recent "pivots" we saw.

So the last strong bounce we had below the PDL zone will be my first downside target.

And the last strong rejection we had above the PDH will be my first upside target.

Here's a quick example of that. (Will make a more detailed thread & video on supply & demand zones soon)👇

My largest sized trades will normally be outside of that previous days range.

And I typically try to avoid taking trades inside of the pre market high / low range.

There are some exceptions to these rules but for the most part I'm looking at the charts like this 👇
EMA trends 📝

This is the other major part of my system. These things are so helpful if you know how to use them.

I love these 3 EMA's on the 2 minute chart...

13 EMA 🟡
48 EMA 🟣
200 EMA 🔴

Let's break down how to utilize them 👇
Bullish EMA trend 📈

13 EMA on top 🟡
48 EMA in middle 🟣
200 EMA on bottom 🔴

Good rule of thumb 👇

Above 200 EMA = Bullish
Above 200 + 48 EMA = More Bullish
Above 200 + 48 + 13 EMA = Mega Bullish

Don't fight the trend ⬇️
Bearish EMA trend 📉

200 EMA on top 🔴
48 EMA in middle 🟣
13 EMA on bottom 🟡

Same rule of thumb in reverse👇

Under 200 EMA = Bearish
Under 200 + 48 EMA = More Bearish
Under 200 + 48 EMA + 13 EMA = Mega Bearish

Again... Don't fight the trend ⬇️
Cheat sheet 👇
Avoid trading the areas where these 3 EMA's are bunched up and not aligned in order.

Study this guide for timing momentum. It will help you avoid trades in choppy areas 👇

Risk Management 📝

I am typically looking for pullback entries into a level or EMA, Then risking 1 candle if that level or EMA does not hold.

This is my "Risk 1 , Win multiple" technique. It helps keep all my loses smaller than my wins 👇

You will lose trades! Cutting loses quickly allows you to recover them a lot easier on the next trade 👇
You will lose trades! Do not bag hold your losers👇
Our support & resistance zones show us the bullish vs bearish areas on the chart 👇
Our EMA trends show us direction & momentum 👇

Our chart patterns give us a nice bit of added confirmation when present 👇
