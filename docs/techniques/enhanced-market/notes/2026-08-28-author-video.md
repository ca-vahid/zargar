   continuation to the next zone / into the gap" (IWM, SPY, QQQ, SPCX, MSFT,

   bounce-first with breakouts demanding heavy confirmation.

   into that gap" (IWM/QQQ); AMD short thesis = "gap to fill below". Our only
   gap concept is gap_void (a hazard that kills triggers). We have no
   gap-fill target anchor at all.

   or the gap edge — often < 1R away — on 0DTE options, scalped. Friday's
   ground truth: SPY/QQQ broke and popped ~+0.4% in the first hour, then faded
   red — his style banks the pop; our 30/40/15+runner all-day ladder would
   have round-tripped it. Entry archetype and exit tempo come as a PAIR.
4. **Indices first.** SPY/QQQ/IWM are his #1/#2 setups ("SPY being number one").
   Penny-wide 0DTE spreads. Our funnel effectively never arms index ETFs.
5. **Earnings gaps are untouchable** ("Affirm gapping 11% on earnings — can't

   ~1.0R (often < 0.5%), orders of magnitude stricter than his.
