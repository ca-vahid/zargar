## 3. Ranked next profitability work

1. **Calibrate first-target and full-exit reachability.** Start with the META comparison above and the actual-trade exit cohort. Report target distance relative to initial risk and the available session movement, proportion reaching each rung before invalidation, and net realized payoff. Keep structural and percentage targets separate. This directly addresses giving back an available move while waiting for an ambitious objective.
2. **Compare one source continuation/retest family against the baseline family on the same names.** Use META and ZS as current examples; keep MU's failed confirmed breakout and SNDK/MRNA's untriggered conditions. Explicitly identify continuation, retest, support bounce and resistance rejection. Do not combine them into one win-rate bucket or automatically prefer the author's direction.
3. **Treat entry timing and stop geometry as one coherent definition.** An opening-range stop may be sensible for an opening-range trade but mismatched to a later tight continuation. Predeclare a second as-of structural definition for future observations. Include the trades it excludes and the extra stops it creates. No stop should be selected retrospectively because it produces 3R.
4. **Keep source intent and horizon in the performance comparison.** Separate EnhancedMarket from EvaPanda, fresh ideas from held positions, and intraday breakouts from swing support holds. The AMZN and MSFT examples show how an attractive chart narrative can become a materially different trade when converted into a generic breakout.

These are research priorities, not today's buy/sell instructions. The source cohort is a small case study; historical option bid/ask paths and full candidate admission are unavailable here. Underlying target touches establish chart events, not executable options profit. Today's baseline remains the comparison group.
